    Four centuries of a city, hosted by people who call it home.

    From the Qutb Shahi forts to the glass towers of HITEC City, Hyderabad has always welcomed the world. Asli's hosts grew up in its lanes, its kitchens and its boardrooms, and open each of them to you.

    A little Hyderabad, wherever you are.

    2. Warm the lac over coals, roll it round and set it with glass stones, the way Laad Bazaar has made bangles for centuries. 02 Lac Bangle Making 30–45 min · Price on requestBook now →
    3. Inlay fine silver wire into blackened metal, the Deccan’s own metalcraft, with a master craftsman beside you. 03 Bidri Art Workshop 45–60 min · Price on requestBook now →
    4. Sort, match and knot Hyderabad pearls into a strand of your own to take home. 04 Pearl Jewellery Making 30–45 min · Price on requestBook now →
    5. See how tie-dyed threads become Pochampally’s geometric ikat, then try a few passes on the loom. 05 Pochampally Ikat Experience 30–45 min · Price on requestBook now →
    6. Write your name in flowing nastaliq with a reed pen and ink, guided by a Hyderabad calligrapher. 06 Deccani Calligraphy 30–45 min · Price on requestBook now →
    7. Dappu drummers and folk dancers bring the energy of a Telangana village festival to your venue. 07 Telangana Folk Performance 20–30 min · Price on requestBook now →
    8. The warrior dance of the Kakatiya kings, performed to the drum by dancers in full ornament. 08 Perini Dance 20–30 min · Price on requestBook now →
    9. Singers and drummers tell a Telangana folk epic in song, part ballad and part theatre. 09 Oggu Katha 20–30 min · Price on requestBook now →
