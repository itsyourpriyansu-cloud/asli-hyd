About this experience

A busy trip needs a quiet hour. Reset in Hyderabad is a restorative session combining meditation, breathwork, yoga or mindfulness in a beautiful setting.

No experience is needed. Tell us how you would like to feel afterwards and the session is shaped around that.

Why guests love it

  • A setting that helps

    Green space does half the work.

  • For complete beginners

    Clear guidance, and nothing to get wrong.

  • Fits a busy trip

    A short, restorative break between plans.
