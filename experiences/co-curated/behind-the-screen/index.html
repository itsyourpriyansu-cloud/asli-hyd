About this experience

Hyderabad makes a remarkable share of India’s cinema. Behind the Screen steps inside the city’s film world through sets, production, cinema stories and behind-the-scenes access.

It is built for people who watch the credits, and for anyone curious about how a film is really put together.

Why guests love it

  • India’s film city

    Hyderabad makes a huge share of Indian cinema.

  • How it really works

    Sets, schedules and the people off camera.

  • Stories you won’t read

    A view of the industry from inside.

How it unfolds

  1. 01Arrive at the film worldYour host sets the scene.
  2. 02Walk the setsStreets, palaces and façades, up close.
  3. 03How production really worksWhat it takes to get a day’s filming done.
  4. 04Stories from behind the cameraTales from the crews who were there.

What’s included

  • Behind-the-scenes visit

Best for
