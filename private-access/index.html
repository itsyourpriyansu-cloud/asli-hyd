Private Access

Hyderabad, behind closed doors.

Rare encounters. Remarkable people.

Request Access →See the encounters
  1. 01

    Meet Tollywood

    A private interaction with a leading actor, director, producer or filmmaker from Telugu cinema.

    Spend time with someone who makes Telugu cinema: on a set, in a studio or over a long lunch. Who you meet depends on who is working and willing.

    Best for: Film lovers, media and entertainment guests

    Request this: Meet Tollywood
  2. 02

    Inside Future City

    A privately curated look at Hyderabad’s ambitious next-generation city, its vision, planning and development.

    See the plans and the land for the city Hyderabad is planning next, with someone who can explain the thinking.

    Best for: Investors, planners and business delegations

    Request this: Inside Future City
  3. 03

    Inside Genome Valley

    A specialist-led journey into Hyderabad’s globally significant pharma, biotech and life-sciences ecosystem.

    Laboratories, manufacturers and the people who run them, chosen around your field.

    Best for: Life sciences leaders and research delegations

    Request this: Inside Genome Valley
  4. 04

    Meet Hyderabad’s Founders

    Private conversations with entrepreneurs and business leaders building some of Hyderabad’s most interesting companies.

    What worked, what did not and what they would do again, told without a slide deck.

    Best for: Founders, investors and senior leaders

    Request this: Meet Hyderabad’s Founders
  5. 05

    Coffee with an Insider

    One-to-one conversation with a carefully matched Hyderabad expert, from innovation and business to culture, design, food or the city itself.

    Tell us what you are curious about, and we will find the one person in Hyderabad you should talk to.

    Best for: Anyone with a good question

    Request this: Coffee with an Insider

By request only

Every Private Access experience is individually curated and subject to host availability, permissions and confirmation. Requests can include film and studio access, and documentary or editorial access.

Request Access

How access works

  1. Tell us who or what

    Share who you would like to meet or see, and why it matters to you.

  2. We ask on your behalf

    Each host decides personally. We share what you have told us.

  3. We agree the details

    Date, length, group size and any conditions the host sets.

  4. You are hosted

    An Asli host stays with you, and the conversation stays private.
